box-sizing Comparison

Default: content-box

box-sizing: content-box

width: 200px applies ONLY to the content. Padding and border are ADDED on top, making total wider than 200px.

width: 200px;
padding: 20px;
border: 5px solid;
/* Total = 250px */

Fixed: border-box

box-sizing: border-box

width: 200px applies to the TOTAL element. Padding and border are included inside — total stays exactly 200px.

width: 200px;
padding: 20px;
border: 5px solid;
/* Total = 200px */
